# The Forge

The Forge is a single-phone conversation card game designed to create playful, romantic, and increasingly meaningful connection between two people.

## Language

**Card**:
A single conversation prompt presented to the players.
_Avoid_: Question, item

**Topic**:
The subject a Card explores and the dimension players can include or exclude from a Deck.
_Avoid_: Category, theme

**Intensity**:
The emotional depth of a Card: light, personal, or deep. Intensity describes a Card without forcing players through fixed stages.
_Avoid_: Level, stage

**Card Type**:
The interaction a Card asks for: question, choice, or challenge.
_Avoid_: Format, mode

**Deck**:
The shuffled sequence of Cards available under the currently selected Topics.
_Avoid_: Question pool, list

**Send to Back**:
Move an unopened Card to the end of the current Deck without consuming it.
_Avoid_: Skip, discard

**Discard**:
Consume an opened Card and advance to the next Card in the Deck.
_Avoid_: Shuffle, send to back
